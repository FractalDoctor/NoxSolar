NoxSolar

Solar Panel Planner
"How much free energy is falling on your roof right now?"

NoxSolar puts solar panel planning in your pocket. Everything you need to calculate your solar resource, estimate panel yield, and project financial returns — all in one place, on your phone, standing in your garden.

Point your phone at the roof. Punch in the numbers. Get answers.

Features

  • NASA POWER satellite data for any location on Earth
  • Compass-based roof orientation — point your phone where your roof faces
  • Visual roof pitch selector with animated diagram
  • Monthly irradiance and yield charts
  • 25-year financial projection with year-by-year table
  • Payback period, ROI, and CO₂ offset calculations
  • Location comparison against global benchmarks
  • Works offline once data is cached
  • Supports GBP, EUR, and USD
  • Light and dark mode
  • No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads
